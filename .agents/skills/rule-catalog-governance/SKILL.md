## 2. 3-3-3 Naming, Aliases & Diagnostic Decoupling

1. **3-3-3 Grammar**: `FAMILY(2-5)-TOPIC(3)-SEQ(3)` from `001` (exceptions: `ARCH-DEC-002`, `ARCH-DSP-002`, `GOV-RTC-002`, `NAM-JRG-002`, `SIM-FLAT-002`).
2. **Glossary & Aliases**: `topic-catalog.ts` maps <!-- METRICS:BEGIN glossary-count -->**113 项**<!-- METRICS:END glossary-count --> across <!-- METRICS:BEGIN topic-count -->**33 个**<!-- METRICS:END topic-count --> canonical topics (`normalizeTopicCode`, `toCanonicalRuleId`); `aliases.ts` resolves Tier 1 $\to$ `LEGACY_RULE_ALIASES` $\to$ `toCanonicalRuleId` (`DISP` $\leftrightarrow$ `DSP`).
3. **Decoupling**: Analyzers emit raw AST issues; `semantic-correlation.ts` deduplicates <!-- METRICS:BEGIN overlap-cluster-count -->**11 个**<!-- METRICS:END overlap-cluster-count --> overlap clusters (`block > warn > info > pass`).
